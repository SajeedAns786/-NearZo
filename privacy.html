Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

NearZo B2B Tyre & Automotive Marketplace ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Platform").

1. Information Collection

We collect information to verify dealer eligibility, prevent spam, and facilitate trade connections between tyre buyers and sellers. The information we collect includes:

  • Personal & Business Details: Name, mobile phone number, business/shop name, shop address, and city.
  • Authentication Data: Mobile number used for One-Time Password (OTP) verification.
  • Trading Data: Sourcing requests, quantities, tyre sizes, pricing quotes, and trade preferences posted on our Platform.
  • Device Information: IP address, device type, OS type, and app usage stats.

2. Mobile Number Usage & SMS

Your mobile number is the primary identifier for your NearZo business account. We use your mobile number to:

  • Authenticate and secure your account via SMS OTP.
  • Provide real-time updates regarding bids, accepted quotes, and network alerts.
  • Enable direct click-to-call or click-to-WhatsApp connections between buying dealers and wholesale suppliers once a trade quote is accepted.

3. OTP Authentication

To ensure that only legitimate tyre dealers access the network, all registrations require verification via a One-Time Password (OTP). We utilize secure, industry-standard authentication systems (including Google Firebase Auth) to verify your device and telephone number during login.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store your account credentials, business listings, and transaction queries on secure cloud databases (using secure systems with restricted access protocols). We take reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural measures to guard your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Sharing of Information

NearZo is a B2B matching service. We share information only under these specific terms:

  • Dealer Connections: When you accept a price quote on the Platform, your contact number and business name are shared with the matching dealer to facilitate deal closure.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with verified third-party tools (like Firebase, SMS gateways, and cloud providers) to operate Platform features. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality terms.
  • Legal Mandate: We may disclose information if required to comply with Indian laws or court orders.

6. User Rights

You have the right to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your account data. You can delete your account directly through the settings panel in our mobile application or by emailing us at support@nearzo.in.

7. Google Play Store Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with Google Play Developer Policies, including rules regarding prominent disclosure of personal and sensitive user data. We do not sell user data to advertising grids, nor do we access your device contacts or photo libraries without your explicit permissions.

8. Policy Updates

We may modify this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the modified Privacy Policy.
