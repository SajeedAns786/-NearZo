Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about the NearZo tyre marketplace platform.

NearZo is a modern B2B mobile tyre marketplace that connects tyre dealers, retailers, wholesalers, distributors, and automotive businesses. It enables users to locate stock, request pricing, and trade tyres quickly through a trusted local dealer grid.
It's simple:
1. A buyer posts a tyre requirement (size, quantity, and brand preference).
2. Verified local suppliers receive a notification and submit their price quotes.
3. The buyer compares the quotes side-by-side.
4. The buyer accepts the best offer.
5. NearZo shares direct phone/WhatsApp contact information to arrange delivery or pickup.
Yes, currently all of NearZo's core services, including posting requirements, receiving quotes, and connecting with local dealers, are completely free of charge. Future premium features or subscription models will be announced well in advance with clear billing terms.
Once you post a sourcing request on the NearZo mobile app, it is instantly broadcast to all verified wholesale dealers in your coverage area. Wholesalers respond directly through their app interface, and their quotes will appear in real time on your request screen.
You can contact a supplier as soon as you review their quote and press "Accept Quote" on the app screen. The app will immediately display their phone number and a direct link to message them on WhatsApp so you can coordinate shipping or invoicing.
We are currently active in Navi Mumbai, Panvel, Kamothe, Kharghar, and Taloja. However, we are rapidly expanding our dealer network and will be launching in other major hubs across India soon.
Yes, security is a core priority. We use secure OTP authentication via Google Firebase to verify user mobile numbers during registration. We do not sell your personal data, and business communications are handled on secure database channels.
