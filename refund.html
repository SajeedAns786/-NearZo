Refund Policy

Home / Refund Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

NearZo B2B Tyre & Automotive Marketplace operates as an intermediary portal for business owners to source tyres and auto parts. Please read our policy on billing and refunds below.

1. Free Core Services

Currently, NearZo is completely free for all tyre dealers, wholesalers, retailers, and distributors. We do not charge fees to register, create sourcing requests, submit price bids, or view supplier contact details.

Since no payment parameters or gateways are active on the Platform, there are no transaction fees, subscription fees, or billing amounts that would warrant a refund.

2. Future Paid Services

Should NearZo introduce premium subscriptions, listing ads, or pay-per-lead models in the future, these services will be accompanied by explicit and separate billing, cancellation, and refund terms.

Any future paid features will require your clear opt-in and acceptance of the corresponding pricing rules prior to any charge being levied.

3. Off-Platform Transactions

NearZo only facilitates business matching. The actual transaction, invoicing, purchase payment, and delivery agreements are completed offline and directly between the respective tyre dealers.

  • We do not process payment transfers between buyers and sellers.
  • We are not responsible for, nor can we issue refunds or settle claims for, issues related to product quality, stock shortages, delivery delays, or payment defaults that occur during direct dealer trades.

4. Billing Concerns and Support

If you receive any suspicious communication claiming to represent NearZo and requesting payments, please do not complete the transfer and report it immediately to our support desk.

For inquiries, billing disputes (in the event of future premium service launches), or reports of fraudulent activity, please contact our support team:
