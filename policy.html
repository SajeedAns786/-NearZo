Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

NearZo is a B2B marketplace that helps tyre dealers, automotive retailers, distributors, and wholesalers connect with nearby businesses for sourcing products and inventory.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Mobile phone number
  • Shop name
  • Owner name
  • City and state
  • Business type
  • WhatsApp availability status
  • Device notification token

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Verify users through OTP authentication
  • Display business information to facilitate marketplace transactions
  • Send notifications about requirements, responses, and deals
  • Improve app functionality and user experience

3. Information Sharing

NearZo does not sell user data.

Business contact information may be shared with another dealer only after a deal is accepted within the application.

4. Data Security

We use Firebase services provided by Google to securely store and process application data.

5. Third-Party Services

NearZo uses:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Expo Notifications

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

6. User Rights

Users may request correction or deletion of their account information by contacting us.
