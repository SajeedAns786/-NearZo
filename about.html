Company Overview

Solving Tyre Sourcing Digitally

NearZo is a B2B tyre marketplace built from the ground up to solve urgent tyre sourcing challenges for dealers and automotive businesses in India.

Historically, tyre retailers and parts dealers have had to make dozens of phone calls everyday just to check local availability for client orders. NearZo changes this. By wrapping the local network of tyre wholesalers, distributors, and shops into a single mobile interface, we make locating and buying tyres lightning fast, completely transparent, and hassle-free.
